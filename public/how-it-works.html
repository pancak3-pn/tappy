Common questions.

Does the person tapping need an app?

No. The selected page opens in the phone's browser.

Will Tappy work with every phone?

Tappy works with NFC-enabled smartphones. NFC must be available and enabled on the device.

Can the destination be changed later?

Yes. The destination can be updated while the same physical Tappy card remains in use.

What can the card open?

It can open a managed contact profile, social page, review link, map, menu, booking page, portfolio or website.

Does Tappy require internet access?

Yes. The phone needs mobile data or Wi-Fi to load the selected destination.
